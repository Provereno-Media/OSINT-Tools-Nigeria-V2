# OSINT-Tools-Nigeria

<img src="https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/7/79/Flag_of_Nigeria.svg" alt="Nigeria flag" width="120"/>

A curated list of free and publicly accessible OSINT resources for investigations related to Nigeria. Most resources are available in English. A listed website is not necessarily a public search of personal records: read each access note before using it.

![Last updated](https://img.shields.io/github/last-commit/Provereno-Media/OSINT-Tools-Nigeria-V2?label=Last%20updated)
**Languages:** Primarily English  
**Coverage:** Federal and state-level Nigerian sources, supplemented by international tools with a specific Nigerian use case.  
**Validation note:** Resource landing pages were reviewed on 2026-09-27. Interactive searches, individual records, and the flag image were not independently tested in every case. Recheck access conditions before relying on a result.

## Open Data portals

- [National Bureau of Statistics](https://www.nigerianstat.gov.ng/) — Official socioeconomic indicators and statistical reports; English; public access.
- [NBS Microdata Catalog](https://microdata.nigerianstat.gov.ng/index.php/catalog) — Official survey metadata and dataset catalog; English; access to individual microdata may have additional conditions.
- [Central Bank of Nigeria: Data & Statistics](https://www.cbn.gov.ng/rates/) — Official exchange rates and macroeconomic and financial series; English; public access.
- [SEC Capital Market Data](https://www.sec.gov.ng/for-operators/keep-track-of-capital-market-data/) — Official market statistics, publications and fund data; English; public access to listed materials.
- [Central Securities Clearing System: Daily Price](https://www.cscs.ng/market-information/daily-price/) — Nigerian exchange prices, symbols and market information; English; public access.
- [National Assembly](https://nass.gov.ng/) — Official bills, parliamentary proceedings and legislative documents; English; public access.

## Legal Entities

- [CAC Public Search](https://icrp.cac.gov.ng/public-search/) — Official search for registered companies and business entities; English; public search page.
- [CAC Beneficial Ownership Register](https://bor.cac.gov.ng/) — Official information on persons with significant control of registered entities; English; public entry page; verify the fields returned by an individual search.
- [SEC Registered Operators](https://sec.gov.ng/for-investors/find-a-registered-operator/) — Official list and search for regulated capital-market operators; English; public access.
- [Nigerian Exchange: Listed Companies](https://ngxgroup.com/exchange/trade/equities/listed-companies/) — Official directory of exchange-listed companies; English; public page, although automated text extraction was unreliable.
- [NASD](https://nasdng.com/) — Regulated market information for unquoted public companies and securities; English; public market pages.

## Maps

- [Kaduna Geographic Information Service](https://kadgis.kdsg.gov.ng/) — Official Kaduna land-administration and geospatial service; English; public information, but do not assume title records are freely searchable.
- [GRID3 Nigeria](https://grid3.org/geospatial-data-nigeria) — Third-party geospatial datasets covering settlements, boundaries, population and infrastructure; English; public data pages.
- [OpenStreetMap: Nigeria](https://www.openstreetmap.org/relation/192787) — Community-maintained Nigerian boundaries and map features; multilingual; public access; not a cadastral ownership register.
- [Office of the Surveyor-General of the Federation](https://osgof.gov.ng/) — Official national surveying and geospatial information; English; public website, with dataset availability to be checked separately.
- [Nigerian Oil Spill Monitor](https://oilspillmonitor.ng/) — Map and incident information based on NOSDRA oil-spill data; English; public access; environmental map, not a land-title register.

## Vehicles

### Land

- [FRSC Number Plate Verification](https://nvis.frsc.gov.ng/VehicleManagement/VerifyPlateNo) — Official Nigerian vehicle-number-plate verification form; English; public page; results were not tested with a plate.
- [NAICOM Motor Insurance Verification](https://mycar.naicom.gov.ng/verify) — Official vehicle-insurance status check by plate number; English; public form; results were not tested.
- [Nigerian Insurance Industry Database](https://askniid.org/) — Industry-operated motor-insurance verification platform; English; public entry page.

### Maritime

- [NIMASA Ship Registration](https://nimasa.gov.ng/what-we-do/ship-registration/) — Official ship-registration rules and guidance; English; public information, not a confirmed public vessel-owner search.
- [Equasis](https://www.equasis.org/) — International ship and company safety information useful for Nigerian vessels; English; free access, ⚠️ registration required.

### Aviation

- [Nigeria Civil Aviation Authority](https://ncaa.gov.ng/) — Official aviation regulations, operator information and notices; English; public information, not a confirmed searchable aircraft-owner registry.
- [ADS-B Exchange](https://www.adsbexchange.com/) — Third-party live aircraft-position service for checking movements over Nigeria; English; public community platform; coverage depends on receivers, while some data products are paid.

## People

- [COREN](https://coren.gov.ng/) — Official engineering regulator with information about registered practitioners and approvals; English; public website.
- [Shine Your Eye](https://www.shineyoureye.org/) — Independent directory of Nigerian elected representatives and public contact information; English; public access; confirm profiles against official records.
- [OrderPaper Nigeria](https://www.orderpaper.ng/) — Independent parliamentary monitoring and legislator-related reporting; English; public access.
- [OpenSanctions PEP datasets](https://www.opensanctions.org/datasets/peps/) — Third-party politically exposed person data with Nigerian source coverage; English; public dataset information; a match is not proof of wrongdoing.
- [Medical and Dental Council of Nigeria](https://mdcn.gov.ng/) — Official professional regulator and licensing information; English; public website; an unrestricted individual lookup was not confirmed.

## Public procurement

- [Bureau of Public Procurement](https://bpp.gov.ng/) — Official procurement regulator, notices, policy documents and links to procurement services; English; public access.
- [Nigeria Open Contracting Portal](https://nocopo.bpp.gov.ng/) — Official federal procurement planning, tenders, awards and contract information; English; public citizen entry page.
- [Open Contracting Partnership: BPP dataset](https://data.open-contracting.org/en/publication/64) — Third-party downloadable NOCOPO-derived contracting data in JSON, Excel and CSV; English; public access; inspect documented data-quality issues.
- [Open Treasury Portal](https://opentreasury.gov.ng/) — Official payment and budget-performance reports for checking public expenditure; English; public access; spending reports are not tender notices.
- [Open Contracting Partnership: Budeshi dataset](https://data.open-contracting.org/en/publication/37) — Third-party historical Nigerian contracting data available for download; English; public access; ⚠️ publisher updates ended in 2021.

## WHOIS

- [NiRA](https://nira.org.ng/) — Official registry information for Nigeria’s .ng domain space and registrars; English; public information; the previously listed direct WHOIS endpoint was not reachable during review.
- [ICANN Lookup](https://lookup.icann.org/) — Official registration-data lookup using RDAP where available; English; public access; registrant details may be redacted.
- [AFRINIC WHOIS](https://afrinic.net/whois.html) — Official African internet-number-resource registration lookup for IP addresses and ASNs; English; public access; this is not a .ng domain-ownership registry.
- [MXToolbox WHOIS](https://mxtoolbox.com/whois.aspx) — Third-party domain-registration lookup; English; public web form; displayed fields depend on the registry.
- [DNSDumpster](https://dnsdumpster.com/) — Third-party DNS and host discovery for investigating a Nigerian domain’s infrastructure; English; free public tool; DNS data does not establish legal ownership.
- [crt.sh](https://crt.sh/) — Public certificate-transparency search by domain or organization; English; public access; certificates provide leads, not proof of domain ownership.

## Contributing

Before adding a resource, check that its page resolves to the described service, that a useful free access path exists, and that its description distinguishes a public search from general agency information. Add each resource to its primary category only.
